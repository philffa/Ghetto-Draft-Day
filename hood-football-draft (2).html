Turn Notifications

A quick honest note: I can't send real texts, emails, or Facebook messages myself — there's no phone number, email account, or server behind this page, just a browser. But these two options send a real push notification to a phone even when the browser is closed. Set up at least the first one for every team.

Option A · ntfy (recommended, 1 minute, free, no signup)
Install the free ntfy app (ntfy.sh, on iOS/Android/desktop). Each team below has a unique topic pre-filled — tap "Open topic" on your phone and hit Subscribe in the app. When it's your pick, this page pings that topic and your phone buzzes, tab or no tab.
Option B · Your own webhook (for real texts/email/Facebook)
Want an actual SMS, email, or Facebook Messenger ping? Make a free Zapier, Make, or IFTTT automation that starts with a "Webhook" trigger and ends with "Send SMS / Send Email / Post to Messenger" — then paste the webhook URL it gives you below. This page will POST {team, pickNumber, round} to it the moment it's your turn.
